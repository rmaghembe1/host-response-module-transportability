# Hybrid / No-APC Target Audit

## Purpose

This document starts the focused financial-first audit of journals that may allow publication without an APC through a subscription/hybrid route, before evaluating fully open-access journals that depend on uncertain waiver outcomes.

## Current author/APC context

All planned authors are Tanzania-based, and Reuben S. Maghembe is the corresponding author. This may improve eligibility for some waiver programmes, but a journal with a confirmed no-APC subscription route remains financially safer than a fully open-access journal with discretionary waiver uncertainty.

## Immediate recommendation

The first formal guideline checks should focus on hybrid/subscription journals, especially **Clinical Microbiology and Infection**, **Diagnostic Microbiology and Infectious Disease**, **Journal of Microbiological Methods**, **Immunology**, and **Genes and Immunity**. Fully open-access journals such as BMC Genomics and BMC Medical Genomics remain scientifically attractive but should stay on hold until full APC waiver or coverage is confirmed.

## Interpretation boundary

Financial targeting does not alter the manuscript framing. The manuscript remains a fixed-module transportability analysis of host-response programmes, not diagnostic classifier discovery, diagnostic model validation, gene rediscovery, module redefinition or causal validation.
