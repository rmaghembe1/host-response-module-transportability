Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled **“Site-aware discovery and external transportability of bacterial- and viral-associated host-response modules in public infection transcriptomes”** for publication as an Original Article in *Clinical Microbiology and Infection*.

This manuscript presents a fixed-module host-response transcriptomic transportability analysis. Using GSE211567 as a site-aware discovery cohort, we defined bacterial- and viral-associated host-response modules and then projected the locked modules into the independent GSE73461 cohort without gene reselection, module redefinition, weight tuning or diagnostic model training. The study identifies host-response programmes with expected-direction external behaviour, especially viral/interferon-associated modules and a bacterial mitochondrial respiration/OXPHOS module, while retaining careful interpretation boundaries.

We believe the work is suitable for *Clinical Microbiology and Infection* because it addresses infectious-disease host-response biology using public clinical transcriptomic datasets and provides a reproducible framework that complements classifier-centred infection transcriptomics. The manuscript is not presented as diagnostic classifier discovery, diagnostic model validation, clinical implementation evidence or causal validation.

All contributors acknowledged in the manuscript, where applicable, have agreed to their inclusion. The work is original, has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal.
