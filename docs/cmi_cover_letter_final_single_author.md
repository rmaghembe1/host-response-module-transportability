Dear Editor,

Please consider my manuscript entitled **“External transportability of bacterial- and viral-associated host-response modules: a site-aware public transcriptomic cohort study”** for publication as an Original Article in *Clinical Microbiology and Infection*.

This manuscript presents a fixed-module host-response transcriptomic transportability analysis. Using GSE211567 as a site-aware discovery cohort, I defined bacterial- and viral-associated host-response modules and then projected the locked modules into the independent GSE73461 cohort without gene reselection, module redefinition, weight tuning or diagnostic model training. The study identifies host-response programmes with expected-direction external behaviour, especially viral/interferon-associated modules and a bacterial mitochondrial respiration/OXPHOS module, while retaining careful interpretation boundaries.

I believe the work is suitable for *Clinical Microbiology and Infection* because it addresses infectious-disease host-response biology using public clinical transcriptomic datasets and provides a reproducible framework that complements classifier-centred infection transcriptomics. The manuscript is not presented as diagnostic classifier discovery, diagnostic model validation, clinical implementation evidence or causal validation.

The Instructions for Authors have been read, and I agree to comply with the journal’s requirements. I confirm that the work is original, has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal.

No dedicated article-processing-charge funding is available for this work. Therefore, submission is intended through the standard subscription/non-open-access publication route unless full open-access coverage is confirmed.
