## Waiver-search implication

All listed authors are based in Tanzania. This should be considered explicitly during journal screening because some publishers determine APC-waiver eligibility based on the corresponding author's country, while others consider whether all authors or all author institutions are located in eligible Research4Life countries.

## Financial constraint

The manuscript has no APC funding. A journal should be considered practical only if one of the following is true:

1. It has a no-APC publication route.
2. It is hybrid/subscription and permits standard publication without open-access charges.
3. It offers a confirmed 100% APC waiver for the corresponding author from Tanzania.
4. It offers a confirmed waiver/discount agreement through Tanzania-relevant arrangements such as EIFL/COTUL, Research4Life-linked agreements or institutional/publisher agreements.
5. It allows a full waiver request before or during submission and the publisher confirms that full waiver is realistic.

## Screening priority

The revised screening order is:

1. Financial feasibility.
2. Waiver/no-APC route certainty.
3. Scientific scope fit.
4. Journal reputation, indexing, speed and formatting practicality.

## Interpretation boundary

The author/APC profile affects journal targeting only. It does not change the scientific framing of the manuscript, which remains a fixed-module transportability analysis of host-response programmes rather than diagnostic classifier discovery, diagnostic model validation, gene rediscovery, module redefinition or causal validation.
